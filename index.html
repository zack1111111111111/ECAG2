ECG Interpretation

ECG looks at cardiac electrical patterns, not only pulse speed.

A heart rate value can look normal while the electrical rhythm is abnormal. ECG adds waveform morphology, beat timing, and conduction pattern, which helps detect irregular rhythms, conduction abnormalities, and early arrhythmia signals.

Heart Rate How fast?

Useful for tracking beats per minute, but limited for understanding rhythm quality.

ECG How healthy?

Shows electrical activity, rhythm regularity, QRS morphology, and early risk patterns.
